Keep the constraints visible

Practical questions.

Where do my recordings and transcripts go?

Audio is processed locally, not uploaded to a transcription service. Transcripts are saved on this computer for the app’s history. Exports go to the location you choose. Anyone who can access your Windows account may be able to read these files. Copying text places it on the Windows clipboard; your Windows clipboard history or sync settings also apply.

How long does transcription take?

It depends on your recording and CPU. Long files can take longer than their playback time. Each file is limited to six hours to keep resource use bounded. You can watch progress or cancel processing. This release uses the bundled base multilingual model, not a cloud service or GPU accelerator.

How accurate is the text?

It is machine-generated, not a verified transcript. Noise, overlapping voices, music, accents, and silence can cause omissions or invented words. Check important names, numbers, and wording against the recording before sharing. Speaker identification is not included.

Which file should I export?

TXT is plain text for notes and documents. SRT and VTT include timestamps for caption tools and video players. Caption timing and wording may need review before publication.

Does portable mean nothing is saved?

No. You do not need to install the app itself, but it saves transcript history and preferences locally. Keep the full extracted application folder together; moving the executable alone leaves its engine and model behind.
